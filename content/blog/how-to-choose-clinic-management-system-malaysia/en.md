To choose a clinic management system in Malaysia, start with your daily work: patient records, the queue, medicine and billing. Then check where the data is kept, what the price includes, and who will set it up and train your staff.

This 2026 checklist works for GP, dental, specialist, aesthetic and TCM clinics. Use it to compare any clinic system in Malaysia and to plan your demo questions.

## What should a clinic management system do?

Whatever kind of clinic you run, check these basics first:

- **Patient records and EMR:** full visit history, diagnoses, prescriptions and notes, easy to find at the next visit.
- **Medication tracking and allergy alerts:** a warning before prescribing a medicine the patient is allergic to, plus batch and expiry on every dispense.
- **Queue number calling:** numbers shown on a TV and called by voice, for rooms, the pharmacy and the payment counter.
- **Appointments with WhatsApp reminders:** patients get a confirmation and a reminder, and can confirm or reschedule with one tap. This helps cut no-shows.
- **Billing and panel claims:** bills, receipts and panel claims in one place, so each claim is easy to follow up.
- **Hardware:** label printers for medicine labels, receipt and MC printing, and X-ray machine connection if you have one.

Then match the system to your clinic type:

| Clinic type | Check especially |
|---|---|
| GP | Fast queue, dispensing and panel claims |
| Dental | Dental charts, X-ray connection and appointments |
| Specialist | Appointment calendar and full visit history |
| Aesthetic | Treatment packages, photos and signed consent |
| TCM | Herbal prescriptions and repeat visits |

## Does your clinic need LHDN e-Invoice?

Not every clinic does. Since 1 September 2026, e-Invoice is voluntary for businesses with annual sales under RM 3 million.

Still, pick a system that can submit e-Invoices to LHDN through MyInvois. Then you are ready if your sales grow or you decide to start. Check with your accountant or LHDN for your own clinic.

## Where is patient data kept, and who can see it?

Patient records are sensitive, so ask direct questions:

- **Servers:** which country are they in? You should get a clear answer.
- **Encryption and backups:** is saved data encrypted, and are backups kept in more than one place?
- **Staff permissions:** can you set what each role sees? A cashier does not need consultation notes.
- **Activity tracking:** is every staff action recorded? You should see who opened a record, gave a discount or issued a refund, and when.

Speed matters too. Ask the vendor to run a real report during the demo, such as this month's collections by doctor. If it is slow with demo data, imagine it with years of records.

## How much does a clinic system cost in Malaysia?

Prices depend on the features, branches and hardware you need, so compare what each price includes. Clinic systems are usually sold as a monthly plan or for a one-off fee.

With a one-off fee, ask about yearly maintenance, upgrades and whether you need your own server. With a monthly plan, ask whether the price rises per user or per branch.

Either way, check that the price covers:

- Installation and hardware setup
- Staff training
- WhatsApp reminders and e-Invoice support
- Backups, updates and support after you go live

Training matters. A system your staff find hard to use will slow your counter down.

## Questions to ask during a clinic system demo

1. Can you show one full patient visit, from registration to payment?
2. What happens when a doctor prescribes a medicine the patient is allergic to?
3. Can patients confirm or reschedule from the WhatsApp reminder?
4. How are panel claims tracked and reported?
5. Can it submit e-Invoices to LHDN through MyInvois when I need it?
6. Will it work with my label printer, number calling system and X-ray machine?
7. Where are the servers, and how are backups kept?
8. Can I limit what each role sees, and check who did what?
9. How fast does a monthly report load?
10. What does the price include, and what costs extra?
