A clinic queue system gives each patient a queue number at registration, then calls it on a TV with a voice announcement. The wait gets shorter and calmer: patients walk in the moment a room is free, and nobody shouts names.

Here is how clinic queue management in Malaysia works, step by step.

## How does a queue number calling system work in a clinic?

1. **Registration.** The front desk registers a new patient or opens a returning patient's record.
2. **Queue number.** The patient gets a number, for example A023, and takes a seat.
3. **Waiting.** Patients relax while a TV shows each number as it is called.
4. **Calling.** When the doctor is ready, one tap calls the next number. The TV shows "A023, Room 2" and a voice announces the call.
5. **Consultation.** The patient walks straight to the right room.
6. **Pharmacy and payment.** Afterwards, the same number is called at the pharmacy and payment counters.

One number takes the patient from start to finish.

## Why does number calling make the wait shorter?

A queue system does not make consultations faster. It removes the dead time between them.

- **No hunting for patients.** A number on screen, with a voice call, is hard to miss.
- **Fewer missed turns.** Patients who step out briefly can check the TV when they return.
- **A quieter front desk.** Patients see the queue moving, so fewer come to ask "how long more?"
- **More privacy.** Full names are not announced to the whole room.

The result is shorter gaps between patients and a waiting room that feels calm and fair.

## How do you mix walk-ins with booked appointments?

Many clinics in Malaysia see both walk-ins and booked patients. The queue runs best with one simple rule that everyone follows:

1. Booked patients who arrive on time go in at their booked time.
2. Walk-ins fill the gaps between appointments, in number order.
3. Keep some open time in each session, so walk-ins are not pushed back all day.
4. Put up a short notice at the counter that explains the rule, so nobody feels skipped.

Whatever the rule, the front desk must see bookings and the walk-in queue together. Then staff can tell each patient where they stand.

## How WhatsApp keeps patients updated

WhatsApp is a simple way to help booked patients arrive on time.

- **Confirmation.** Send a confirmation as soon as the appointment is made.
- **Reminder.** Send a reminder before the visit.
- **One-tap reply.** Let patients confirm or reschedule with one tap, so unused slots go to someone else.

Fewer no-shows and late arrivals make the day's queue easier to predict. Walk-ins benefit too, because early reschedules open up time for them.

## How queue calling works with several doctors or rooms

With two or more doctors, each call must say where to go, not only who is next.

- Show the room with every number, for example "A023, Room 2", and label each door to match.
- Use the voice announcement too, so patients looking at their phones still hear their turn.
- Every device should match: the doctor's tablet, the counter PC and the TV.
