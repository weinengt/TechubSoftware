To reduce no-shows at a salon, beauty centre or spa, use two things together: WhatsApp appointment reminders and a booking deposit. Reminders stop customers from forgetting, and a deposit gives them a reason to turn up or reschedule early.

Here is how hair salons, beauty centres and spas in Malaysia can set up both, and word a deposit policy that feels fair.

## Why do customers miss appointments?

No-shows are usually not on purpose. Each common reason has a simple fix:

| Why it happens | What fixes it |
|---|---|
| They forgot, because they booked weeks ago | A WhatsApp reminder before the visit |
| Plans changed, and calling to cancel felt awkward | One tap to confirm or reschedule |
| Skipping costs them nothing | A small booking deposit |
| They weren't sure of the time or place | A clear confirmation right after booking |

Every no-show is an empty chair or room. Your stylist, beautician or therapist loses that time, and the slot is hard to fill at short notice.

## How to automate salon appointments with WhatsApp

Sending reminders by hand works for a few bookings a day. Once you get busy, it eats front desk time and some messages get missed. An appointment system sends them for you, in a flow like this:

1. The customer books online or at the counter.
2. A WhatsApp confirmation goes out straight away, with the service, date, time, staff name and address.
3. A reminder goes out before the visit. A day ahead gives the customer time to reschedule, and gives you time to refill the slot.
4. The customer taps to confirm, or chooses to reschedule.
5. Your front desk sees who has confirmed and only follows up on the rest.

Keep each message short and friendly, with the customer's name. Make rescheduling easy too. A moved booking beats an empty slot.

## How does a booking deposit system work?

A booking deposit is a small amount the customer pays to hold their slot. On the day, it comes off the bill, so customers who turn up pay nothing extra.

With a payment gateway, customers pay the deposit online when they book. Depending on the gateway, they can use online banking (FPX), a card or an e-wallet. The money goes into your business's own merchant account.

You don't need a deposit for every booking. Consider one for:

- Long or costly services, such as colour, keratin, facials or spa packages
- Peak slots, such as weekends and evenings
- First-time customers who book online
- Customers who have missed a booking before

## How to write a fair deposit policy

Customers should understand your policy before they pay. Cover these points in plain words:

- **Amount:** how much the deposit is, and that it comes off the final bill.
- **Notice:** how many hours before the visit a customer can reschedule or cancel.
- **Late changes and no-shows:** what happens to the deposit.
- **If you cancel:** a full refund, or the deposit moved to a new date.
- **Where it shows:** the booking page, the WhatsApp confirmation and the counter.

Here is sample wording you can adapt. Change the amount and notice time to suit your business.

- A RM 30 deposit holds your booking and comes off your bill on the day.
- Need to change? Reschedule at least 24 hours before, and your deposit moves to the new time.
- If you cancel later than that or don't come, the deposit is not refunded.
- If we cancel, we refund it in full.

Be firm, but stay kind. For a real emergency, you can still move the deposit to a new date. Train your staff to explain the policy the same way every time.

This is general guidance, not legal advice. For how deposits are treated in your accounts and tax, check with your accountant.
